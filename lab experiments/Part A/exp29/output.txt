--- Sensitivity Analysis to Extreme Outliers ---
           Metric Without_Outlier With_Outlier
Range       Range          200.00      2380.00
IQR           IQR           82.50        82.50
Variance Variance         4143.33    527343.33
SD             SD           64.37       726.18

Discussion:
- Range, Variance and SD are extremely sensitive to outliers because they incorporate squared or boundary values.
- IQR is robust to extreme values as it focuses on the middle 50% of the distribution.
